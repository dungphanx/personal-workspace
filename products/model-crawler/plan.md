# Model Crawler – plan

Snapshot 2026-11-26. Requirements live in the linked issues; this page only orders them. Product page: [README.md](README.md).

## Vision

A steady, rule-abiding feed of great printable models into PrintLab.

## Goals

- Versioned and reviewable
- Exports flow into the PrintLab catalogue

## Non-goals

- Paid models
- Bypassing access controls
