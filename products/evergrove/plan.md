# Evergrove – plan

Snapshot 2026-11-25. Requirements live in the linked issues; this page only orders them. Product page: [README.md](README.md).

## Vision

Every family can find their loved ones and every cemetery runs its records and reservations online.

## Goals

- One codebase chosen and carried to a first public release
- Trustworthy sign-in for families and boards
- Grave records and reservations managed online

## Non-goals

- Online payment
- Running two codebases long term
