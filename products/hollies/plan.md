# Hollies – plan

Snapshot 2026-11-17. The detailed backlog lives in the product's own (private) repository; this page keeps the product-level direction. Product page: [README.md](README.md).

## Vision

A daily companion that keeps one person honest about time, priorities and habits, and makes it easy to get back on track.

## Goals

- Used every day on a phone without friction
- Recover quickly after off-track days
- Native apps with real notifications once the web app has proven the loop

## Non-goals

- Accounts, cloud sync or social features for now
- Coaching content or AI advice

## Milestones

| Milestone | Scope | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M1 – MVP | Time log, top 3, habits, insights, daily loop (check-in, morning, weekly review, nudges), web app | Done |
| M2 – Back on track | Rename to Hollies and UX fixes (done), recovery mode, small reliability fixes, confirmed habit targets | In progress |
| M2b – Health habits | Counters and PIN lock (done), what is improving vs the last 4 weeks, weight log, counters in the on-track score | In progress |
| M3 – Native apps | Store identifiers, real notifications on Android and iOS, store builds | Later |

## Risks

- Data lives only on one device: losing the device or clearing the browser loses the history

## Open decisions

- Habit targets and study details (owner).
- Store app identifiers for Android and iOS (owner).
